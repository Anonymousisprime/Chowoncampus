Delivery Agent Privacy & Rating Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how ChowOnCampus handles delivery-agent information and how customer ratings are treated in relation to agent performance and fees. It supplements the General Privacy Policy.

1. Information we handle

Depending on your account and deliveries, we may process your name and username, account/contact details you provide, online/offline availability, assigned and completed delivery records, relevant customer and restaurant order details, customer ratings and feedback, payout records, support messages, and security logs.

2. GPS and online status

If you allow location access, your delivery location is collected for delivery coordination while your account is marked online. Location updates are intended to stop when you go offline. Your location may be shared with authorised administrators and, when needed for a delivery, with the customer or restaurant involved. Keep location sharing enabled only when you are ready to accept deliveries, and report any location or account issue to support.

3. How we use and share information

Information is used to assign and fulfil orders, provide pickup and delivery details, show delivery status, calculate and record earnings, display ratings, investigate complaints, provide support, maintain safety, and prevent fraud or misuse. Customer information is provided to you only as needed for an assigned delivery. Use it only to complete that order or handle an authorised support issue; do not save, share, or reuse it for unrelated purposes.

4. Customer ratings

Customers may rate their delivery experience and leave feedback. Ratings can help identify good service, recurring delivery issues, and cases that need support review. A rating is one signal, not proof by itself that an agent did something wrong. We may consider the number of ratings, written context, order records, and relevant information from the agent before taking action. Fake ratings, harassment, or requests to manipulate ratings are not allowed.

5. How ratings affect your fees and earnings

Current standard pay: the current platform rule is ₦100 for each completed delivery. Customer ratings do not automatically reduce this base amount, and a low rating by itself does not authorise a deduction from money already earned.
  • Good ratings: help demonstrate reliable service and may be considered when the platform reviews performance or future incentive programmes. No bonus or higher fee is guaranteed unless it is separately announced in writing.
  • Mixed or low ratings: may lead to a fair review, feedback, or coaching if there is a pattern or a specific substantiated complaint. A single rating should not automatically change your per-delivery pay.
  • Repeated, substantiated service problems: may affect access to future assignments or account status after review and notice. Any future change to the fee rate or introduction of rating-based bonuses must be clearly communicated before it takes effect; it will not be applied retroactively to completed deliveries.
  • Disputes: if you believe a rating is unfair or relates to an order issue outside your control, contact support with the order ID and explanation so the matter can be reviewed.

These are the current policy principles, not an automated pay formula. The platform does not currently apply a star-rating-to-fee table. If a formal rating-based pay scheme is introduced, agents should receive the thresholds, calculation method, effective date, and review process in advance.

6. Delivery-agent complaints, refunds and hearings

Customer refunds are handled by the Ultimate Admin. A delivery agent is not automatically responsible for funding a customer refund and does not automatically lose earned delivery pay because a complaint or low rating is received. A deduction, fine, or other disciplinary action must not be imposed solely on the basis of an unverified complaint or rating.

If a complaint alleges that an agent caused a delivery problem, ChowOnCampus may review the order timeline, assigned instructions, delivery location details, messages, available evidence, and the agent's explanation. Where a student supplied an incorrect or incomplete address, that fact must be considered; responsibility is decided case by case and is not automatically assigned to the agent.

If a warning, fine, or suspension is issued, the agent may appeal and request a formal hearing by contacting Support at 09169398221. Include the case/order ID, the reason for challenging the decision, and any supporting evidence. The request will be recorded for the Ultimate Admin. The hearing request does not automatically reverse or pause the decision; it will be reviewed and may result in the decision being upheld, changed, or overturned.

7. Security, retention and privacy requests

Protect your password and verification codes, sign out on shared devices, and report suspected account access promptly. Information may be kept as reasonably needed for delivery operations, earnings and payment records, dispute resolution, security, and legal obligations. Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of personal information, or ask about deletion or restriction. Identity verification and certain record-retention exceptions may apply.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service changes. For questions, privacy requests, or a rating dispute, contact ChowOnCampus Support, call 09169398221, or message WhatsApp Support, or email chowoncampus@gmail.com.

This is a practical policy draft, not legal advice. The operator should confirm the payment model, rating workflow, location handling, retention practices, and applicable legal obligations before commercial launch.
