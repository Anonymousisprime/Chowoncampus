Restaurant Partner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how ChowOnCampus handles information connected with restaurant partner accounts. It supplements the General Privacy Policy. It is a practical policy draft and should be reviewed against the platform's actual operations before commercial launch.

1. Information we handle

Depending on your account and use of the service, we may process your restaurant or business name, account username, contact details supplied to us, menu and item information, prices, order status, customer reviews and ratings, pickup location or map pin, support messages, and account/security logs. Do not submit personal information that is not needed to operate your restaurant account.

2. How information is used

We use this information to create and secure partner accounts, display your restaurant and menu, receive and manage orders, coordinate pickups and deliveries, calculate order totals, display customer ratings, resolve complaints, provide support, prevent fraud or misuse, and maintain operational records.

3. What customers and delivery agents can see

Customers may see your restaurant name, menu, prices, published pickup details, and ratings or reviews intended to be displayed on the platform. A delivery agent assigned to an order may receive the restaurant pickup location and the order details needed to collect it. Do not put private personal details in public menu descriptions or review replies.

4. Location and map pins

Your restaurant location may be supplied using device GPS or a manually placed map pin. It is used to help customers find the restaurant and agents collect orders. Check that the pin is accurate and that you are authorised to publish that location. Location visibility may be available to customers, assigned agents, and authorised administrators where needed for platform operations.

5. Ratings and reviews

Customer ratings may be used to show restaurant performance and help customers make choices. We may review reports of fake, abusive, or otherwise inappropriate reviews. Ratings should not be manipulated or used to post private information. This privacy policy does not promise a particular ranking, sales level, or rating outcome.

6. Sharing and service providers

Relevant information may be shared with customers, the delivery agent assigned to an order, authorised ChowOnCampus administrators/support staff, and service providers needed to host and operate the platform, process payments, or provide security and communications. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users or the service. We do not sell restaurant account information to advertisers as a business model.

7. Security and account responsibilities

Keep your password and verification codes private, limit dashboard access to trusted staff, sign out on shared devices, and tell support promptly if you suspect unauthorised access. Use customer details only to prepare and fulfil the relevant order or handle an authorised complaint. Do not copy, publish, sell, or reuse customer contact or location information for unrelated marketing.

8. Retention and your choices

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, resolve order/payment disputes, maintain security and business records, and meet legal obligations. Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct your account information, or request deletion or restriction where appropriate. We may need to verify your identity and retain certain records for legitimate legal or operational reasons.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. For privacy questions or requests, contact ChowOnCampus Support, call 09131999590, or message WhatsApp Support, or email chowoncampus@gmail.com.

This is general information, not legal advice. The platform operator should verify the actual data flows, access permissions, service providers, retention periods, and applicable Nigerian data-protection obligations.
