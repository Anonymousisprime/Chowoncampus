Delivery & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the basic expectations for orders made through ChowOnCampus. It should be read with our Terms of Service.

Demo status: The current website checkout says no real payment is collected by the form. This page does not create a payment or refund facility that is not currently available.

1. Before placing an order

Check the restaurant, items, quantities, displayed prices, delivery details, and estimated fees before submitting an order. Menu availability and prices may change, and an order may need restaurant or platform confirmation.

2. Delivery information

Enter a clear hostel or residence, building/room where relevant, and a nearby landmark. If you choose to share GPS coordinates, check that the pin is accurate and that you are comfortable sharing it for delivery. You can use manual directions instead where supported.

3. Delivery estimates and delays

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. Preparation delays, campus access, weather, traffic, inaccurate directions, or agent availability can affect arrival time. If a delivery is delayed or the order status does not look right, contact support and provide the order ID.

4. Cancelling or changing an order

If you need to cancel or change an order, contact support as soon as possible and include the order ID. Whether a change or cancellation can be made may depend on whether the restaurant has started preparing the food or the delivery has been assigned or started. Do not assume a cancellation has succeeded until you receive confirmation.

5. Missing, incorrect, or damaged orders

Contact support promptly with the order ID and a clear explanation of what is wrong. Include relevant details, but avoid sharing passwords, verification codes, payment PINs, or unnecessary personal information. We may need to ask the restaurant or assigned agent for information to investigate.

6. 48-hour return, replacement, and complaint window

Customers must contact ChowOnCampus Support within 48 hours of receiving their order to request a return, replacement, or review for a refund. Include the order ID and explain the issue clearly. Where useful and safe, provide relevant photos of an incorrect, missing, damaged, or otherwise problematic order. We will review the request and may ask the restaurant or assigned delivery agent for information.

Submitting a request within 48 hours does not automatically guarantee a refund or replacement; the outcome depends on the circumstances, applicable law, and the payment/order arrangements actually in place. Because prepared food is perishable and can present food-safety risks, do not send food back or consume food you believe is unsafe unless support gives safe, specific instructions. Keep the item and packaging only when it is safe and reasonable to do so. This policy does not limit any consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

7. Student refund and FoodBack rules

Students must report a missing, incorrect, damaged, unsafe, or disputed order within 48 hours of receiving it. Include the order ID and a clear explanation. The 48-hour period is a reporting deadline, not an automatic refund promise.

  • FoodBack: where the Ultimate Admin approves a FoodBack remedy, the stated policy is 50% of the eligible food value as FoodBack credit. It is not an automatic cash refund.
  • Non-refundable portion: the current rule states that 30% is not refunded. The current rule does not yet explain how the remaining 20% is allocated. The Ultimate Admin must state the complete approved breakdown in writing before a final amount is communicated; no one should assume that the unaddressed 20% is refundable or forfeited.
  • Student cancellation: a student cancellation before the restaurant begins preparation does not qualify for the 50% FoodBack remedy under the current rule. Any other payment or cancellation resolution must be reviewed and confirmed by the Ultimate Admin; cancellation is not complete until confirmed.
  • Evidence and review: ChowOnCampus may check order status, preparation status, restaurant and agent records, delivery details, messages, and relevant photos. Food should not be returned unless Support gives safe, specific instructions.
  • Payment status: the current public checkout notice says it is a demo and does not collect real payments through that form. No automated cash refund or FoodBack issuance is promised by this page. If a payment was made outside the website, keep the receipt and contact the payment recipient and Support. An approved decision must be processed separately and recorded.

8. Who is responsible?

Students: provide accurate delivery details, remain reachable, check the order before confirming receipt where practical, and report problems within 48 hours. If incorrect or incomplete directions cause a delivery problem, the case is reviewed using the order record and the agent's account; responsibility is not decided automatically.

Restaurants: keep menu availability accurate, accept only orders they can fulfil, prepare the accepted items as described, and promptly report unavailable items. A restaurant that accepts an order while an item is unavailable or cannot fulfil it may be investigated. Where an approved refund is caused by a restaurant issue and the restaurant has already received settlement, the restaurant is responsible for funding the approved restaurant-related refund, subject to the case decision and applicable law.

Delivery agents: follow the assigned delivery instructions, protect order information, and report delays or delivery obstacles promptly. Agents do not automatically fund customer refunds, and no deduction from earned delivery pay or automatic fine is made solely because a complaint or low rating is received. Any alleged agent misconduct is investigated separately with an opportunity for the agent to respond.

9. Decisions, appeals and hearings

The Ultimate Admin reviews refund requests and decides whether a remedy is approved, based on the available evidence and applicable law. A restaurant or delivery agent who receives a warning, fine, or suspension may challenge the decision and request a formal hearing by contacting Support at 09169398221 and providing the case/order ID, reasons, and any supporting evidence. The request will be recorded for the Ultimate Admin to review. A hearing request does not automatically reverse or pause the original decision; the outcome may uphold, vary, or overturn it after fair consideration.

10. Contact support

Use the ChowOnCampus Support page, call 09169398221, or message us on WhatsApp, or email chowoncampus@gmail.com. Include your order ID where applicable.

This is a general operational draft, not legal advice. The operator should confirm cancellation windows, escalation steps, and any future refund process before launching real-money orders.
