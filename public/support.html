Common help topics

I forgot my student password

Open the Forgot Password page and follow the SMS verification steps. If SMS recovery is unavailable, contact support.

I can't log in or create an account

Contact support and include the phone number registered to your account. Never send your password or SMS verification code.

I need help with an order or delivery

Share your order ID and a short explanation so the support team can understand the issue. Do not share payment PINs or passwords.

What should I include in a support message?

Describe the issue, the page you were using, and any error message shown. For order issues, include your order ID.
