FoodBack credits are promotional rewards, not a bank balance. They cannot be withdrawn or transferred. You can use FoodBack credits at checkout when your wallet covers the full order total. Every FoodBack-paid order is sent to the ultimate admin for review; the restaurant is notified only after approval. If an order is rejected, the credits are returned to your wallet.
