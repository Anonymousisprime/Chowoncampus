Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ChowOnCampus (“ChowOnCampus”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our campus food discovery, ordering, account, and delivery features. Please use the service only if you understand this policy.

Important: The current website checkout identifies itself as a demo and does not collect real payments through that form. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment PINs, or full card details to support.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details: information such as your name, phone number, account role, and authentication-related records that you submit when creating or using an account.
  • Order and delivery details: items ordered, order ID and status, hostel or residence, building, room, landmark, and information needed to coordinate delivery.
  • Location: if you choose to share GPS coordinates or a map pin, we may use that location to help identify a delivery point. Your browser/device should ask permission before sharing current GPS location. You can enter an address manually instead.
  • Ratings and messages: ratings, reviews, feedback, and information you provide when contacting support.
  • Technical and security information: limited information generated when the service is used, such as request, error, or security logs, where available from our application and hosting providers.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, show menus, process and coordinate orders, help restaurants and delivery agents fulfil orders, provide order updates, display ratings, answer support requests, prevent misuse, troubleshoot the service, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Location and delivery-agent tracking

Sharing a student's current GPS location is optional; manual delivery details may be used instead. Location information supplied for an order may be shared with the relevant restaurant or assigned delivery agent to fulfil that order. Where agent online-location tracking is enabled, it is intended to operate while the agent is marked online for delivery work. Avoid sharing a location that is not needed to complete a delivery.

4. When information may be shared

We may share only relevant information with the restaurant preparing an order, the delivery agent assigned to it, authorised ChowOnCampus administrators/support staff, and service providers that help host or operate the platform (for example, database, hosting, or SMS verification providers). We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights. We do not promise that every feature or provider is available at all times.

5. Security

We use measures intended to protect information, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a unique password, keep your verification codes private, log out on shared devices, and report suspected account access to support promptly.

6. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to operate accounts, fulfil and resolve orders, maintain security and business records, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by data type and operational or legal needs. We may retain limited records when needed to investigate disputes or prevent abuse.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, including Nigeria's data-protection framework, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information, and to raise an objection or complaint. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons. To make a privacy request, contact support using the details below and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

8. Children and campus users

The service is intended for people who are legally able to create and use an account under applicable rules. Do not submit another person's personal information or location without appropriate permission.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service and its features change. The date at the top indicates the latest stated revision. Continued use after an update means the updated policy may apply to subsequent use, subject to applicable law.
